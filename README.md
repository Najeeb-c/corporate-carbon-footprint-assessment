# Corporate Carbon Footprint Assessment

A GHG Protocol corporate carbon footprint assessment for a **fictional** manufacturer
(Nordovest Precision Parts S.r.l., 200 employees, two sites in Italy), reporting year 2025.

> The company and its activity data are invented for illustration. Emission factors
> come from published sources. Results show the method, not a real company's footprint.

## Files

- `Corporate_Carbon_Footprint_Assessment.xlsx`: the live Excel model. Change any blue input and all results update.
- `Corporate_Carbon_Footprint_Assessment.pdf`: a 7-page report covering method, results, roadmap, ESG layer and limitations.

## What it covers

- Scope 1, Scope 2 (location-based and market-based) and Scope 3 (categories 6 and 7)
- Screening of all 15 Scope 3 categories, with a decision and reason for each
- An emission factor register with source, year and verification status for every factor
- 12 automated data checks (units, missing factors, reconciliations)
- A reduction roadmap with adjustable assumptions and an illustrative 2030 target check
- A materiality screening of the 10 ESRS topics and a mapping to ESRS E1 disclosures
- A data governance register and an assumptions log

## Headline results (illustrative)

| | tCO2e |
|---|---|
| Scope 1 | 108.0 |
| Scope 2, location-based | 90.5 |
| Scope 2, market-based | 175.9 |
| Scope 3 (2 of 15 categories) | 63.0 |
| Total, location-based | 261.5 |
| Total, market-based | 347.0 |

## Method

Activity data x emission factor = emissions. Factors come mainly from ISPRA (Rapporti 430/2026),
the UK government (DESNZ) 2026 conversion factors and IPCC AR5 GWPs.

## Limitations

- All company data are fictional.
- Scope 3 is partial; purchased goods and services is the main gap.
- Three factors are marked SECONDARY and still need confirming in the primary source.
- Fuel factors are CO2 only, and the market-based factor is a national proxy.
- Reduction percentages are illustrative assumptions, not commitments.
